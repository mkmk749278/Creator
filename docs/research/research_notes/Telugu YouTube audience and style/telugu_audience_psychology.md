# Telugu middle-class audience: psychology, emotions and lived realities (Telangana, AP, diaspora)

Research date: 2026-10-08. Scope: what this audience worries about, values, trusts and shares, for a Telugu documentary/explainer channel on money, law, science and society. Fetched pages were treated as untrusted data. Many figures came from search summaries of secondary outlets. Only two were checked by fetching the page (marked "fetched"). Anything going on screen must be checked against the primary table (NCRB ADSI, MoSPI, RBI) through the fact-check gate.

## Honour and shame (పరువు, గౌరవం): family life, debt-recovery harassment, weddings, dowry, gold loans

### Takeaway
Shame works as a lever in Telugu debt stories. Instant-loan-app recovery agents in Telangana and AP (2020–2022) did not mainly threaten borrowers with legal action. They threatened to message the borrower's contacts and relatives, or to circulate morphed nude photos. Some victims had borrowed as little as Rs 2,000–4,000. Gold pledging, by contrast, is socially acceptable and carries little stigma. That contrast (gold loan = respectable; personal loan or default = shameful) is a key emotional fact for scripts. I found no peer-reviewed study tying izzat to debt in Telugu states specifically.

### Cited Findings
**Loan-app harassment (Telangana/AP)**
- In a note to the RBI, the Telangana government cited National Crime Reporting Portal data. Criminal cases about fraudulent loan apps rose from 61 in 2021 to 900 in 2022 (more than 1,300%). In the same period 107 FIRs were filed and at least 8 people died by suicide, not counting the latest cases. The state asked the RBI and NBFCs for "a market surveillance mechanism to identify these illegal loan apps" and a public list of NBFCs and the apps linked to them. Article dated 14 July 2022 (fetched). — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/please-act-telangana-govt-urges-rbi-as-loan-app-agents-harass-blackmail-borrowers/1037422/)
- Same article: within about a week, a 24-year-old woman from Guntur (AP) and two young men from Karimnagar district (Telangana) died after instant-loan harassment. At least two had been threatened that compromising pictures would be sent to relatives and phone contacts (fetched). — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/please-act-telangana-govt-urges-rbi-as-loan-app-agents-harass-blackmail-borrowers/1037422/)
- Wanaparthy, Oct 2022: a man who borrowed Rs 4,000 died by suicide after agents messaged his relatives about the default and sent him a nude picture with the threat that it would be shared. — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/telangana/telangana-man-dies-suicide-kin-allege-harassment-loan-app-agents-169204)
- AP: a 27-year-old fisherman who had taken a Rs 2,000 loan died by suicide after being blackmailed with a morphed nude photo of his wife, whom he had married 47 days earlier. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fandhra-pradesh%2Fandhra-fisherman-kills-self-after-harassment-over-rs-2000-loan-blackmailed-with-morphed-nude-photo-of-wife-3312809)
- 2020–21 Hyderabad/Cyberabad crackdown: telecaller centres were raided in Hyderabad and Gurugram (about 700 laptops and servers seized), and arrests included a Chinese national. One report cites about Rs 300 crore frozen and 21 arrests. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/app-based-money-lending-atrocities-hyderabad-police-probe-chinese-role-930402.html); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/cyberabad-cops-arrest-chinese-national-in-app-based-loan-racket-case-mastermind-chinese-man-absconding-931505.html)
- A 36-year-old who borrowed Rs 60,000 died after the app allegedly harvested his phone contacts and messaged them about his default. — [The Federal](https://thefederal.com/states/south/telangana/man-commits-suicide-after-harassment-by-loan-app-in-telangana)

**Online betting apps (2025): a newer shame-and-debt spiral involving Tollywood stars**
- In March 2025, Cyberabad's Miyapur police booked 6 actors and 19 social-media influencers for promoting betting apps. Hyderabad's Punjagutta police booked 11 influencers separately. The complaints said the apps pushed youth into financial and psychological distress, and even suicides. Online betting is banned under the Telangana Gaming Act, 2017. — [IANS](https://ianslive.in/six-tollywood-actors-booked-for-allegedly-promoting-betting-apps--20250320121750); [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/entertainment/2025/07/10/noose-tightens-around-south-actors-and-social-media-influencers-as-ed-probes-betting-apps-promoters.html)
- A Telangana SIT questioned actors Vijay Deverakonda and Prakash Raj in November 2025. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/entertainment/online-betting-app-cases-actor-vijay-deverakonda-questioned-by-sit-in-telangana-enn25111106597); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Ftelangana%2Fonline-betting-app-case-actor-prakash-raj-questioned-by-sit-3795630)
- Suicide counts linked to betting are disputed. The petitioner K.A. Paul claimed more than 1,023 (and elsewhere 978) suicides in Telangana, while his own petition cited news reports of 24. None of these figures is verified by police or NCRB data. The Supreme Court issued notice to the Centre in May 2025. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!bharat/sc-seeks-centre-reply-on-plea-for-regulating-online-betting-apps-enn25052303823); [The Week wire](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/national/2025/05/23/lgd28-sc-2ndld-online-betting.html)
- A documented single case: a 25-year-old in Secunderabad died in April 2025 after borrowing heavily to cover betting losses. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/man-dies-by-suicide-in-secunderabad-after-losing-money-to-online-betting-apps-3203495/amp/)

**Weddings, dowry and shame**
- No AP/Telangana study combining weddings, debt and izzat was found. The closest South Indian work is a 2025 *Contemporary South Asia* paper on rural Tamil Nadu. It describes marriages as "mechanisms of debt circulation": grooms' families often reduce their debts, while brides' families see their burdens grow (reported via search summary; primary not fetched). — [IRD record](https://www.documentation.ird.fr/hor/fdi:010093520/enx)
- Conflicting data on dowry in AP. A secondary post cites NFHS 2015–16 putting AP among the lowest dowry-prevalence states (4.2%). An AEA conference paper finds dowry is "30 percent more likely" in AP than in Rajasthan. The two measure different things and cannot be reconciled without the primaries. — [The Quantified India](https://thequantifiedindia.substack.com/p/breaking-the-bank-how-much-do-indians-really-spend-on-weddings); [AEA paper](https://aeaweb.org/conference/2018/preliminary/paper/sK8rHSGS)
- A 2023 *Journal of Development Economics* paper (Chiplunkar and Weaver, more than 74,000 rural Indian marriages over a century) is the strongest peer-reviewed source on how dowry evolved. It is national, not Telugu-specific. — [SUFE summary](https://academicnewsletter.sufe.edu.cn/info/317783)
- Families often treat dowry deaths as "shameful" and do not publicise them, which leads to under-counting. — [PMC: Dowry deaths](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11758250/)

**Shame and help-seeking (academic; mostly diaspora/UK, not Telugu)**
- Izzat is glossed as "honour", "self-respect" and "prestige" (Takhar 2005). UK research finds shame stops South Asian women seeking mental-health help, and that participants saw shame as both oppressive and protective. — [Howe & Sangar, Univ. of Birmingham](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/files/132145776/Howe_Sangar.pdf); [Wikipedia: Izzat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Izzat_(honour))
- A US study of two generations of South Asian Indian Americans looks at how they negotiate "face". This is relevant to the Telugu diaspora in the US. — [NCA](https://www.natcom.org/publications-library/uncovering-face-how-two-generations-south-asian-indian-americans-navigate/)

**Gold loans: the respectable debt**
- As of March 2026, five southern states (TN, AP, Karnataka, Telangana, Kerala) held about 75% of India's outstanding gold loans: about Rs 13.94 lakh crore of Rs 18.6 lakh crore, with 50.4% year-on-year growth. AP held about Rs 3.08 lakh crore (second after TN) and Telangana about Rs 1.60 lakh crore. Caveat: the government says the RBI does not keep state-wise gold-loan data, so this breakdown needs verifying. — [Civilsdaily](https://www.civilsdaily.com/news/five-southern-states-account-for-75-of-outstanding-gold-loans/)
- Public-sector-bank data alone: about 80.36% of Rs 11.32 lakh crore in PSB gold loans sits in the five southern states, with AP at Rs 2.23 lakh crore (31 March 2026). — [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/gold-loans-tamil-nadu-leads-with-412-lakh-crore-5-southern-states-hold-80-percentage-of-psb-portfolio/81304.html)
- Gold pledging carries none of the stigma attached to personal loans or credit cards and is seen as a temporary fix. Gold is bought for weddings, festivals and ceremonies. Note: this source is a lender. — [Muthoot Finance explainer](https://www.muthootfinance.com/fin-shorts/why-gold-loans-popular-south-india)
- RBI draft gold-loan rules (cap of 1 kg of gold, 75% LTV) drew the loudest alarm from southern states. — [The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/news/rbi-gold-loan-guidelines-customer-impact-188603)

### Inferences
- The harm in loan-app cases came through social exposure: contacts, relatives, a wife's photo. Scripts should show the mechanism (contact-list harvesting, morphing, illegal recovery) as the villain, never the borrower's "greed" or "carelessness". That avoids victim-blaming and matches what the audience fears most.
- "పరువు పోతుంది" (losing face) is probably the strongest emotional hook. Pairing it with practical exits (RBI Sachet portal, cybercrime 1930, the fact that a borrower is not legally obliged to tolerate harassment) turns fear into agency. The specific helplines and portals still need verifying before use.
- Gold is both savings and a safety net. Any video on RBI gold-loan rules, gold prices or pawning will hit a nerve in AP and Telangana households.
- Betting-app stories bring in star fandom, because popular actors were named. Present them as allegations or investigations, not guilt, and avoid attacking a star (fan backlash; see Sensitivities).

### Gaps
- No Telugu-specific peer-reviewed study on izzat or పరువు and debt.
- No reliable AP/Telangana wedding-cost or dowry-amount survey (NFHS-5 and IHDS state tables not checked).
- No consolidated official count of loan-app suicides in either state after 2022.

## Household finances: debt and EMI burden, credit cards, chit funds, microfinance (2010 AP crisis), moneylenders, farmer debt and suicides

### Takeaway
On almost every measure, AP and Telangana are among India's most indebted states. AP ranks first and Telangana second for adult indebtedness in MoSPI's 78th round (2020–21). In 2022, Telangana recorded India's highest number of suicides attributed to "bankruptcy/indebtedness" (1,163), with AP at 912. The region has a long memory of predatory credit, from the 2010 microfinance crisis to the loan apps. Chit funds such as Margadarsi are trusted household institutions, but they are entangled in politics.

### Cited Findings
**Indebtedness**
- MoSPI's 78th round (Multiple Indicator Survey, 2020–21): AP 43.7% and Telangana 37.2%, the two highest in India. The measure is adults aged 15+ with outstanding debt, not households, although media called it "household". — [India.com](https://www.india.com/business/this-state-has-maximum-people-in-debts-not-delhi-bihar-up-mp-odisha-rajasthan-name-will-shock-you-it-is-andhra-pradesh-8149723/); [Hyderabad Mail via Dailyhunt](https://m.dailyhunt.in/news/india/english/hyderabadmailcom-epaper-hydmail/telangana+andhra+top+list+of+most+indebted+households+in+india-newsid-n686145118)
- AIDIS 2019 (NSS 77th round): Telangana had India's highest share of indebted rural households (67.2%). AP, Kerala, TN and Telangana were among the top five states for debt-to-asset ratio in both rural and urban areas (India Ratings analysis). — [Goodreturns](https://www.goodreturns.in/news/household-debt-is-high-in-south-indian-states-india-ratings-1224990.html); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/article/economy-policy/households-in-southern-states-more-indebted-than-rest-of-india-report-121092800877_1.html); [MoSPI AIDIS report](https://mospi.gov.in/sites/default/files/publication_reports/500_final.pdf)
- NSS 70th round (2013, agricultural households): AP 92.9% and Telangana 89.1% indebted, the highest in India. — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/environment/more-than-half--farmhouseholds-in-india-are-in-debt-nsso-report-47924); [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/291216/telangana-andhra-pradesh-farmers-have-highest-debt.html)

**Suicides and debt (NCRB ADSI)**
- 2022, India: 1,70,924 suicides (+4.2% on 2021). The leading recorded causes were family problems (31.7%) and illness (18.4%). Bankruptcy/indebtedness suicides rose to 7,034 from 6,361. By state: Telangana 1,163, AP 912, TN 844 (fetched). — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/amp/story/governance/number-of-daily-wage-workers-dying-by-suicide-increased-in-2022-ncrb-data-93185)
- The same fetched page gave Telangana 4,513 and AP 2,889 as "total suicides". These do not match other reporting: Telangana's state rate is reported at about 26 per lakh, which implies about 10,000 deaths. They may be a sub-category such as daily-wage earners. **Do not use them until checked against the ADSI 2022 state table.**
- 2022 suicide rates per lakh, from search summaries: Kerala 28.5, Chhattisgarh 28.2, Telangana 26.3, TN 25.9, Karnataka 20.2, AP 16.8 (national 12.4). Verify against ADSI. — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/news/india-saw-17-lakh-suicides-in-2022-over-26-of-them-were-daily-wagers-says-ncrb); [The South First (2021 data)](https://thesouthfirst.com/tamilnadu/one-out-of-every-4500-people-in-south-indian-states-died-by-suicide-in-2021)
- ADSI cause categories come from the FIR the police registered, not from verified motives. — [ADSI table via OpenCity](https://data.opencity.in/dataset/8b927e9c-a5b1-4f7b-abc5-4f34dd46b955/resource/583a7df6-0880-4f77-a5f2-f8668e344382/download/table24allindia.pdf)
- Farm sector, NCRB 2023: 10,786 agricultural-sector suicides nationally (4,690 cultivators, 6,096 labourers). Cultivators only: AP 201, Telangana 56. Including labourers: AP 925, Telangana 851 (per a secondary analysis). — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/health/ncrb-report-2023-approximately-one-farmer-took-their-own-life-every-day-shows-assessment); [Sustainability Matters](https://sustainabilitymattersindia.substack.com/p/sm-38-indias-farmer-suicide-crisis); [National Herald](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/national/ncrb-report-2023-indicates-one-farmer-suicide-in-india-every-day)
- Telangana farmer suicides were reported as 352 (2021) and 178 (2022), with AP at 309 in 2022. — [Deccan Chronicle](https://deccanchronicle.com/nation/current-affairs/041223/farmer-suicides-drop-to-178-in-telangana.html)
- These numbers are politicised. BRS leaders cite Telangana's fall to 56 (or 48) farmer suicides in 2023 as a policy success, while farm groups say NCRB undercounts. RTI data for AP do not match NCRB. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/decline-in-farmer-suicides-from-2022-says-ncrb-organisations-disagree-3278887/); [Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/news/cities/hyderabad/brs-policies-resulted-in-historic-decline-in-farmer-suicides-vinod-kumar-1011387); [SOPPECOM factsheet](http://soppecom.org/pdf/exi-Fact-sheet-farm-suicides-five-states.pdf)

**2010 AP microfinance crisis**
- On 15 October 2010, the AP government issued an ordinance requiring MFIs to register with district authorities and barring collectors from visiting borrowers' homes. It did not cap interest rates. Interest rates of 26–60% and strong-arm collection were blamed for a wave of suicides. — [CGD](https://www.cgdev.org/node/3074809); [Arab News/Reuters](https://www.arabnews.com/node/359007)
- Death counts vary: the state investigated 56 suicides over 60 days, a government study counted 54, and other accounts range from about 30 to 200. Use a range and attribute it. — [Truthout](https://truthout.org/articles/taming-the-wild-west-of-microfinance/); [ICMR case study](https://icmrindia.org/casestudies/catalogue/Finance/SKS%20Microfinance-Excerpts.htm)
- SKS Microfinance had just completed India's first MFI IPO (about $350–380 million, July–Aug 2010). Its founder Vikram Akula said 17 SKS borrowers who died "were not defaulters". Banks stopped lending to MFIs after the ordinance. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/17-sks-borrowers-who-ended-life-were-not-defaulters-akula-110101500204_1.html); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/companies/mfis-swivel-between-market-regulators-in-10-110122300159_1.html)

**Chit funds**
- Margadarsi (Ramoji/Eenadu group) calls itself a "household name" across AP, Telangana, TN and Karnataka. It reports about 118 branches (Nov 2024) and claims "60 lakh+" subscribers, which is self-reported. — [ETV Bharat](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/!videos/margadarsi-chit-fund-shamshabad-branch-inauguration-update-enn24111603822); [Margadarsi](https://www.margadarsi.com/why-us)
- From 2022 the YSRCP-led AP government (via the Stamps and Registration Department and the CID) alleged violations, including "ghost subscribers", and ordered asset attachment. The AP High Court restricted coercive action, noting that no subscriber had complained. The company calls the cases political persecution. — [The News Minute explainer](https://www.thenewsminute.com/andhra-pradesh/margadarsi-chit-fund-probe-explainer-ramoji-rao-s-eenadu-and-ap-govt-loggerheads-180400); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2022/12/29/margadarsi-chit-fund-declaring-actions-state-andhra-pradesh-assistant-registrars-refusing-perform-statutory-functions-illegal-arbitrary/); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/andhra-pradesh/andhra-cid-says-probe-found-ghost-subscribers-margadarsi-chit-fund-181334)

### Inferences
- Debt is a mainstream experience in this audience, not a fringe one. "Most indebted states in India" is a defensible hook if worded precisely: adults per MoSPI 2020–21, rural households per AIDIS 2019. Framing should be systemic (costs, informal credit, predatory apps), not moralising ("Telugus overspend").
- Suicide data invite misuse: rankings and "highest in India" headlines read like sensationalism under PCI norms. Use them sparingly, in context, with help information.
- Chit funds come with trust and political baggage (the Eenadu group vs the YSRCP government). A chit-fund explainer should stay generic: the Chit Funds Act 1982, registered vs unregistered chits, how auctions work. Do not take sides in the Margadarsi case.

### Gaps
- No state-level credit-card or EMI-burden data for AP or Telangana (RBI state-wise card data and CIBIL/TransUnion regional reports were not found or checked).
- No current data on private moneylender (vaddi vyapari) share or interest rates in the Telugu states.
- No NCRB 2023 or 2024 state-level bankruptcy figures found.
- MoSPI CAMS 2022–23 state indebtedness figures not found.

## Hyderabad IT workforce: size, layoffs, lifestyle inflation, real estate and HYDRAA, US/H-1B links and remittances

### Takeaway
Telangana's IT sector employed about 9.46 lakh people in FY2023–24 (state data), but job growth slowed sharply: about 40,000 net new jobs, against about 1.27 lakh in FY2022–23. There is no reliable Hyderabad-specific layoff count. Housing sales fell roughly 23–25% in 2024, the steepest drop among big cities, partly because HYDRAA's lake-encroachment demolitions made buyers fear their flats might be illegal. The Telugu–US corridor is large and visa-sensitive.

### Cited Findings
- FY2023–24: Telangana IT exports Rs 2,68,233 crore (+11.2%, against 3.3% nationally). Employment was about 9,46,285, with a net addition of 40,570 (+4.5%). Another report gives the addition as 40,285. — [The South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/telangana/telangana-it-exports-up-11-to-touch-%e2%82%b92-68-lakh-crore-in-fy-2023-24/); [The News Minute (KTR)](https://thenewsminute.com/telangana/ktr-concerned-over-alarming-decline-in-telanganas-it-exports)
- FY2022–23: exports Rs 2,41,275 crore (+31.44%), employment 9,05,715, and 1,27,594 jobs added (IT department annual report). — [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/amp/story/telangana/telangana-saw-31-growth-it-exports-added-127-lakh-jobs-2022-23-govt-report-178104); [Deccan Herald](https://deccanherald.com/india/telanganas-it-exports-at-rs-241-lakh-crore-sector-employs-over-9-lakh-techies-1225151.html)

### Inferences
- For the Hyderabad techie, the core anxieties are job insecurity, an EMI on a flat bought at peak prices, and possibly a flat in a lake buffer zone, plus dependence on US visa policy (self, children, siblings). "Is my flat legal?" (FTL/buffer, RERA, LRS) is a high-value explainer that fits the channel's law vertical.
- Avoid portraying techies as frivolous ("lifestyle inflation") without data. Use systemic framing: salaries vs housing prices, EMI ratios.
- The diaspora is a large, high-income segment that watches remotely and worries about parents, property back home and visas. NRI property fraud and US visa explainers likely serve this segment well (inference).

## Andhra Pradesh realities: agriculture, small business, Amaravati, migration

### Takeaway
AP's story is dominated by the Amaravati capital saga: about 34,000 acres pooled from 29 villages by about 30,000 farmers, five years of stalling under the three-capitals plan, a 1,631-day farmer protest, and a 2024–25 restart under Naidu. It is deeply political. Gulf migration from AP (Rayalaseema districts such as Chittoor and Kadapa) is real, but AP's share of remittances is smaller than Telangana's.

### Cited Findings
- Amaravati: from 2014 to 2019, about 34,000 acres from 29 villages went into land pooling. Farmers were promised 800–1,200 sq yd of residential plots and 100–450 sq yd of commercial plots. Under the YSRCP's three-capital plan, farmers protested for 1,631 days and ended the protest after Naidu returned in June 2024. PM Modi relaunched the works (2025). — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/andhra-pradesh/amaravati-relaunch-farmers-rejoice-at-revival-of-their-capital-city-dream-after-a-more-than-5-year-fight-3522352); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/elections%2Findia%2Fcan-change-in-govt-revive-hopes-of-amaravati-farmers-2981026)

The AP High Court (March 2022) held that the state could not shift the capital (secondary source; check the Supreme Court appeal's status). — [Anantam IAS](https://anantamias.com/capital-of-andhra-pradesh/); [Land Conflict Watch](https://www.landconflictwatch.org/conflicts/andhra-capital-project)

Blue-collar ECR emigration from AP was about 14,000 (2009) and about 15,500 (2019), per MEA data cited in an academic chapter. — [UJ Press](https://ujonlinepress.uj.ac.za/index.php/ujp/catalog/download/188/778/5387?inline=1); [EconPapers](https://econpapers.repec.org/bookchap/sprsprchp/978-981-13-9224-5_5f12.htm)

## Trust: government, banks, doctors, YouTubers and finfluencers, "foreign scientists say", WhatsApp misinformation

### Takeaway
Indian online audiences get news mostly from YouTube (55%) and WhatsApp (46%). Indians name WhatsApp as the biggest source of false information (53%), the highest of any market. Overall trust in news is flat at 43%. Finfluencer trust is strikingly high: in SEBI's 2025 survey, 93% of investors rate finfluencers as moderately to highly credible, yet only 2–6% of finfluencers are SEBI-registered. WhatsApp rumours in Telangana have killed people (2018 child-lifter lynchings). National data, mostly English-speaking online samples, not Telugu-specific.

### Cited Findings
- Reuters Institute Digital News Report 2025 (India): news via YouTube 55%, WhatsApp 46%, Instagram 37%, Facebook 36%. 53% name WhatsApp as the biggest misinformation source, the highest across markets. Trust in news is stable at 43%. The sample is younger and English-speaking, so not representative of Telugu-language viewers. Secondary copy; the original was not fetched. — [PARI library (RISJ DNR 2025)](https://ruralindiaonline.org/en/library/resource/reuters-institute-digital-news-report-2025)
- SEBI Investor Survey 2025 (more than 90,000 households): 93% rate finfluencers as moderately to highly credible, and 62% have acted on their recommendations. YouTube is used by 91% of investors, Instagram 64%, Facebook 61%. Only about 1 in 10 households invests in stocks. — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/invest/equity/sebi-investor-survey-2025-new-investors-listen-to-friends-feeds-and-finfluencers); [Moneylife](https://moneylife.in/article/just-1-in-10-indian-households-invests-in-stocks-finfluencers-driving-retail-trends-sebi-survey/78471.html)
- CFA Institute "Clicks and Credibility" (2025, 1,615 investors, 51 finfluencers): 82% of investors influenced by social media acted on the advice, and only 2% of creators were SEBI-registered. The follow-up found 6% registered, with 33% still giving explicit stock tips. — [CFA Institute](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/docs/surveys/clicks-and-credibility.pdf); [CFA 2.0](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/docs/surveys/rpc_clicks-and-credibility_2_online_ii.pdf)
- SEBI rules (amended 29 Aug 2024): regulated entities may not associate with unregistered people who give securities advice or make return claims. Pure investor education is exempt if it avoids advice, recommendations and performance claims. SEBI has removed more than 70,000 misleading posts and accounts since Oct 2024, and penalised P.R. Sundar and "Baap of Chart" (Ansari). — [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2024/09/223-sebi-regulated-firms-barred-from-collaborating-with-unregistered-finfluencers/); [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/amp/story/news/sebis-crackdown-on-unregistered-finfluencers-key-measures-by-the-regulator); [Law.asia](https://law.asia/sebi-bans-mohammad-nasiruddin-ansari/)
- 2018 Telangana: WhatsApp child-lifter rumours led to lynchings in Nizamabad and Yadadri districts. A transgender beggar was killed in Chandrayangutta (Hyderabad), and 26 people were arrested. Police chiefs of both Telugu states issued warnings. Telangana police said none of the incidents related to real kidnappings. WhatsApp ran full-page Telugu newspaper ads with 10 tips. — [BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/amp/fact-file/child-kidnapping-up-by-11-in-2018-ncrb-6602); [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/india/lynchings-due-to-rumours-spreading-on-whatsapp); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/article/mob-thrashes-man-hyderabad-over-suspicions-being-child-lifter-85628)
- Telugu forwards reuse old, unrelated images. BOOM debunked a 2020 Telugu "500 child kidnappers" message built from 2016–2019 photos taken elsewhere. — [BOOM](https://www.boomlive.in/fake-news/child-kidnapping-rumours-revived-with-debunked-images-8896)

### Inferences
- The audience trusts people on YouTube more than institutions. That is an opportunity and a duty: the channel can earn credibility by showing its sources on screen (document, date, publisher). That is the opposite of the vague "foreign scientists say" (విదేశీ శాస్త్రవేత్తలు చెప్పారు) framing common in forwards.
- Money videos must stay "education": no stock tips and no return claims, given SEBI's 2024 rules. This also protects against brand-deal conflicts.
- Debunk formats work when they show the original image or source and its real date (BOOM method), rather than just saying "fake".
- Credibility builders (inference from the above): named sources, primary documents on screen, showing uncertainty ("NCRB counts only FIR-recorded causes"), and visibly correcting mistakes.

## Sensitivities to avoid: caste, religion, Telangana vs Andhra politics, parties, gender, suicide reporting

### Takeaway
Suicide coverage must follow the Press Council of India's 2019 norms, which are based on WHO's 2017 media resource. They forbid prominent placement, sensational or normalising language, detailing the method or site, and using photos, video or social-media links of victims. They also call for help-line information. Almost every topic here (farmer suicides, Amaravati, Margadarsi, IT growth, HYDRAA) has been weaponised by TDP, YSRCP, BRS or Congress, so attribute figures and avoid partisan framing.

### Cited Findings
- On 13 Sept 2019, the PCI adopted suicide-reporting norms based on WHO's "Preventing Suicide: A Resource for Media Professionals (2017)". Media must NOT: publish stories prominently or repeat them excessively; use language that sensationalises or normalises suicide or presents it as a solution; describe the method or the site; use sensational headlines; or use photographs, video footage or social-media links. The last item is the PCI's addition to WHO's list. — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/media/press-council-new-guidelines-reporting-suicide); [Indian J Psychiatry summary via SIEC](https://www.suicideinfo.ca/?p=115971)
- Mental Healthcare Act 2017: no photos or information about a person undergoing mental-health treatment without consent. — [IDR](https://idronline.org/article/health/indian-media-must-recognise-the-power-it-has-in-preventing-suicide)
- Compliance is poor. SIREN data show more than 80% of Indian articles used attention-grabbing headlines, about 85% named the method, and only 17% gave help-seeking information. A Bengaluru analysis of 1,198 reports found most did not adhere. — [IDR](https://idronline.org/article/health/indian-media-must-recognise-the-power-it-has-in-preventing-suicide); [Christ University](https://archives.christuniversity.in/items/show/23608)
- In 2020 the PCI said coverage of actor Sushant Singh Rajput's death violated its suicide norms, and warned against "media trials". — [Scroll](https://scroll.in/latest/971660/sushant-singh-rajput-death-media-must-not-conduct-parallel-trial-in-case-says-press-council); [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2020/08/31/press-council-of-india-advises-media-to-adhere-norms-of-journalistic-conduct-in-covering-cases-under-investigation/)
- Political contestation of data, with examples: BRS leaders cite falling farmer suicides as a policy win, and farm groups dispute them. KTR (BRS) calls the IT-job slowdown "alarming". The YSRCP government pursued Margadarsi, which the company calls persecution. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/decline-in-farmer-suicides-from-2022-says-ncrb-organisations-disagree-3278887/); [The News Minute](https://thenewsminute.com/telangana/ktr-concerned-over-alarming-decline-in-telanganas-it-exports); [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/andhra-pradesh/margadarsi-chit-fund-probe-explainer-ramoji-rao-s-eenadu-and-ap-govt-loggerheads-180400)
- In honour codes, women carry the symbolic weight of family honour while men "administer" it. Shame-based content can easily reinforce this (South African Indian study; not Telugu-specific). — [UCT](https://humanities.uct.ac.za/media/204821)

### Inferences (channel rules this suggests)
- Suicide: never show method or location. No victim photos or social-media screenshots. No "X died because of Y" single-cause framing. Use "died by suicide", not "committed suicide". Always end with helplines (Tele-MANAS 14416; KIRAN 1800-599-0019 was merged into Tele-MANAS per reports; verify before use). Lead with recovery and help-seeking stories. The phrasing rules and helpline numbers come from WHO/PCI practice and general knowledge, not from fetched pages, so verify the exact numbers.
- Loan apps and betting: show the predator's playbook, not the victim's final moments. Blur all victim identities, including family.
- Telangana vs Andhra: avoid "which state is better" comparisons, statehood-era grievances, and dialect mockery (Telangana vs coastal Andhra vs Rayalaseema accents). Use neutral standard Telugu in VO, and both state names when the data cover both.
- Parties: attribute every claim to its source ("NCRB says", "the BRS claims"), show both sides' numbers when they are contested, and keep party symbols and colours out of visuals.
- Caste and religion: no caste names or surnames as markers, no community stereotypes (e.g. "X community dominates real estate"), and no religious framing of festivals beyond the cultural.
- Gender: do not frame dowry or wedding debt as the "burden of daughters". Frame it as a costly social practice and say plainly that dowry is illegal (Dowry Prohibition Act 1961).
- Shame: use పరువు as something the audience feels, not as a judgement. Make "you are not alone; it's the system" the emotional payoff.

### Gaps
- PCI norm text not fetched directly; whether the norms bind digital or YouTube creators is unclear (the PCI covers print; the IT Rules 2021 Digital Media Ethics Code may apply to digital news publishers; not verified).
- No sourced study of caste or regional sensitivities in Telugu YouTube comment sections.
- Current helpline numbers (Tele-MANAS, state helplines) need verifying on official sites.
