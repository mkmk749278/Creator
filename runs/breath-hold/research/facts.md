## Science / records
| Claim | Status | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| The urge to breathe comes from rising CO₂, not falling O₂ (hypercapnic drive, central and peripheral chemoreceptors) | Standard physiology | Shown |
| CO₂ forms carbonic acid, so blood pH falls | Standard physiology | Meter values (40→75 mmHg, pH 7.40→7.20) are **illustrative** |
| Involuntary diaphragm contractions late in a hold | Standard freediving physiology | Shown |
| Static apnea, air: **11:35, Stéphane Mifsud, 2009** (AIDA) | Confirmed | Shown, with source line |
| O₂-assisted: **24:37, Budimir Šobat, 2021** (Guinness), after breathing pure O₂ beforehand (allowed up to 30 min) | Confirmed | Shown, labelled "O₂-assisted" |
| Heart rate drops, blood shunted to brain and heart (dive reflex) | True for the dive reflex in general. **Not measured on Vidyut.** | ECG labelled "Illustration: stage మీద ఎవరూ measure చేయలేదు" |
| "Oxygen requirement drops 40–50%" | **No source found.** It's a claim in the narration. | Tag: "Claim in the narration, not measured" |
| Micro-ventilation through the nose | Speculative. The narration says "ఉండొచ్చు" (may be). | Eyebrow: "Possible explanation" |
