matching slot. Each slot lists its time in the final video.

Rules for every prompt:

  would be fake footage of a real person. Use anatomy, abstract scenes or anonymous silhouettes only.
- 16:9, 1920×1080 or larger, 5–10 s, no on-screen text (we add Telugu text ourselves), no logos.
- Log every AI clip you use in the credits/description ("Abstract 3D visuals: AI-generated with <tool>").

Midjourney is for stills (then animate with a slow push-in, or use Runway image-to-video).
