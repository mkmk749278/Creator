## 1. Genshin Impact ◐
- **Fixed:** Android Authority (10 Sep): "A recent patch fixed the Genshin Impact issue". The fix came from the game; no Google GPU driver update was found.
- **Still open:** Android Authority (21 Sep) found heavy stutter after the September patches, caused by ANGLE. Workaround: Developer options → ANGLE Preferences → Genshin → Native. Google has not fixed it.
- The phone passed 40 °C after 5 minutes and still can't hold 60fps.

## 2. HiLight ✅
- Google Store India still says it covers only "favourite contacts are calling or you are talking with Gemini".
- "Messages from favorites" is in **Android 17 QPR2 Beta 6.1** (Droid Life, 29 Sep) on the Pro, Pro XL and Fold.
- Tech Advisor (2 Oct) expects it around a December Pixel Drop. Google has not confirmed this.

## 4. Updates ⚠️
- **1 Sep and 9–10 Sep:** two Pixel 11 hotfixes (CD1A.260905.001.A1 and .B1) at the 1 Sep patch level. Google's notes only say "fixes critical bugs… performance and stability".
- **15 Sep:** the September Pixel Drop (Android 17 QPR1) included a fix for "game lag, choppy framerates, and increased device temperatures".
- ⚠️ **Did Pixel 11 get QPR1?** 9to5Google and Android Authority's supported-phone list say yes. Android Authority's changelog piece and Notebookcheck (16 Sep) say no: Pixel 6–10 only, with Pixel 11 still on the 1 Sep patch. Don't put either version on screen.
- **Standby drain:** no fix yet. Android Authority (21 Sep) reports 2.5–3%/h idle on some routers (Tenda, TP-Link Omada, Fritz!Box). Switching the router to Wi-Fi 5 brings it to about 1%/h. Google hasn't confirmed the bug.
- **Charging and thermals:** no official fix found.
- **October:** no October 2026 Pixel bulletin yet; September is the latest listed. Android 17 QPR3 Beta 1 came out on 2 Oct.

## 6. Practical items (new)
- **Weight:** Pixel 11 197 g, Pro 204 g ("surprisingly hefty"), Pro XL 226 g, Fold 239 g. Both phones have slippery frames (GSMArena).
- **Buttons:** the power button sits above the volume rocker.
- **Camera bump:** the camera bar doesn't wobble on a table.
- **Fingerprint:** ultrasonic, "fast and reliable" (GSMArena).
- **Face unlock:** good enough for banking apps and payments (Android Authority, 26 Sep).
- **Speakers:** stereo, "pleasantly loud and clear" (HotHardware). No loudness test was found.
- **Haptics:** "strong and precise" (HotHardware).
- **Modem:** MediaTek (M90 per HotHardware) replaces Samsung's Exynos.
  - It holds 5G better than Pixel 10 and downloads faster.
  - Uploads still trail the Galaxy S26 Ultra.
  - All tests were on US networks; there is no India network test.
- **Repair:** JerryRigEverything says it is as easy to repair as previous Pixels. No iFixit score was found.
- **Service in India:** 1-year warranty. 20+ walk-in centres, including 4 Google Exclusive ones (Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bangalore) open 7 days a week. Google says 9 in 10 phones are repaired in under 4 hours. Paid pickup is available in Bangalore.
- **SIM:** nano SIM + eSIM on every model. No Jio, Airtel or Vi page lists Pixel 11 yet.
- **In the box:** phone, USB-C cable, SIM tool. **No charger.**
