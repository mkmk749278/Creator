All four models use Tensor G6 with Titan M3, carry IP68 and get 7 years of updates. Every model starts at 256GB.

## Headline changes vs Pixel 10
- Tensor G6: 50% more TPU compute, about 25% faster web browsing and 15% faster app launches. Sources disagree on the process node: 2nm (Android Central) vs 3nm (GSMArena).
- The camera bar is 40% thinner. Pro screens have more than twice the scratch resistance and reach 3600 nits.
- Base storage goes from 128GB to 256GB, and the US price rises with it. The Pro and Pro XL now start at 12GB RAM instead of 16GB.
- 25W Qi2.2 magnetic wireless charging (Pixel 10 had 15W Qi2). The Pro XL gets 45W wired.
- The Pro telephoto is 48MP with 30% more light sensitivity. Instant Night Sight is up to 4.5x faster. The base Pixel 11 has a 48MP main camera with 56% more light sensitivity.
- HiLight is a coloured LED notification light on the Pro, Pro XL and Fold.
- The Fold is about 10% lighter and 1mm thinner, with a gearless hinge. Google calls it "3x more durable".
- New software: Magic Capture, Camera Looks, Gemini Intelligence (multistep tasks across 40+ apps), Sign-to-Text, Rambler and Live Translate dubbing.

## Conflicts and caveats
- One source (Engadget) says a telephoto lens was "added" to the base Pixel 11. The Pixel 10 already had a 5x telephoto, so this claim is wrong.
- Pre-order date: 12 Aug in the main launch post vs 13 Aug in the HiLight post.
- GSMArena lists the Pixel 11 in India at Rs 104,999, which is the 512GB price. Its Pixel 11 Pro price of "$824.99" is the current discounted street price, not the launch price.
- Prices on store.google.com load in the browser after the page opens, so they could not be read directly. The Google Store also still sells the Pixel 10a. No Pixel 11a was found.
