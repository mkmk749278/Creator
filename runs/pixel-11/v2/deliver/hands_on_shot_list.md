# Hands-on shot list (for a future cut, if you can get a Pixel 11 unit)

The external review's top request was **authentic evidence**: the phone in use and photos taken with it.
Only footage you shoot yourself (or have permission to use) can fill this. All of it can be shot on a phone.
Label every clip: `Model | lens/zoom | mode | date | software version`.

## Priority 1: camera (the biggest gap)
| Question | Shoot this | Keep |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday photos | 3 daylight + 2 backlit scenes, 1×, then the same at 5× | Full image + 1 matched crop |
| People | Portrait indoors (window light) and outdoors; one selfie of each | Same person, same day |
| Kids / pets moving | 10 shots of a moving subject indoors, Magic Capture on and off | Keep the failures too |
| Night | Main + ultrawide in a dim street; note Night Sight on/off and capture time | Photo + stopwatch |
| Zoom | One fixed scene at 1× / 2× / 5× / 10× / 30× (base), plus 60× / 120× on a Pro | Same tripod spot |
| Video | 30 s walking (rear + selfie), 15 s low light, a focus change | Uncompressed originals |
| AI tools | Before/after for Magic Capture, Camera Looks, Add Me | Show the original action |

## Priority 2: using the phone
- Outdoor screen in midday sun; scroll at 60 Hz vs 120 Hz (Settings → Display).
- App switching, a 20-minute Genshin / BGMI session with the frame-rate counter and room temperature noted.
- Rambler in Hindi and English, including a mid-sentence language switch; show the delay.
- Proactive Assistance and HiLight in real use (and when they don't appear).
- Fingerprint and face unlock with a banking app; a call and 5G speed test on Jio / Airtel / Vi.
- Charging 0→50% with the charger you actually own (note its wattage and PPS support).

## Priority 3 (Fold, if available)
- Crease under angled light, the hinge opening and closing, pocket size vs a normal phone, two apps split-screen.

When the clips exist, the pipeline can annotate and cut them into the camera, display, performance and software parts without changing the rest.
