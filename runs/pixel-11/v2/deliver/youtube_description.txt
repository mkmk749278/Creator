Pixel 11, 11 Pro, 11 Pro XL and 11 Pro Fold: should you buy one in India? We read 35 reviews from 20 outlets so you don't have to.

We did not test these phones ourselves. Every claim in this video comes from the reviews listed below. Prices: Google Store India list prices and Flipkart prices as checked on 5 Oct 2026; prices change daily. Launch-time bugs and feature availability were re-checked on 5 Oct 2026.

Camera samples shown are Google-published marketing images (Google did not state which model took them). They show the camera at its best, not how often it gets there. Product images: Google.
