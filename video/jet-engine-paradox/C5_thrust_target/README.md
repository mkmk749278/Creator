# C5_thrust_target: Target 81 kN

Session C scene for *The Jet Engine Paradox* (program 201.95–208.44, 6.49 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps). Spec and cues:
`projects/jet-engine-paradox/scenes.md`. Drive: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TmQBj2wdsFRpfk0TEEBRjCfCGD5n04cN/view

The Kaveri blueprint (same drawing as C4) re-inks in 1.1 s while the camera dollies and swings toward side-on. A
dashed white target line rises on the right at 0.9 s ("The goal was huge"). 1.55–3.5 s a bold amber thrust arrow grows
out of the nozzle and strikes the line (white flash); tag `TARGET THRUST 81 kN` at 2.8 s ("eighty one"). At 5.05 s
("with the afterburner on") the afterburner section runs hot and a flame plume with moving shock diamonds grows from
the nozzle.

Cue (word times estimated by character position):
