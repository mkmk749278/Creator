How long can this take?
If the money didn't reach them —
whether it says failed or processing —
the bank must return it,
and the R.B.I. has set a deadline.
Paid a person?
Back by the next day.
Paid a shop or a business?
Within 5 days — 5 normal days,
Sundays included, not 5 working days.
Person, 1 day.
Shop, 5 days.
And if the bank takes longer,
it has to pay you ₹100 for every extra day.
You don't even have to complain.
The bank has to pay it on its own.
That's an R.B.I. rule.
Quick summary, the way I remember it.
Success — the shop got it.
Failed — it didn't go through,
your money comes back on its own,
and you can pay again.
Processing — don't pay again.
If it never reaches the shop,
you get it back within 1 day for a person,
5 days for a shop.
