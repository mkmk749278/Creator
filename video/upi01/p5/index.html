My one rule: never pay again while it says processing.
Because if that first payment turns out to be a yes... congrats,
you just paid ₹840... twice.
Don't want to wait?
Open your app, go to your payment history,
tap that payment, and look for U.P.I. Help.
You can raise it right away.
Paid twice by mistake?
Choose duplicate payment there.
Still not solved?
Complain to your bank.
Bank not fixing it?
Then complain to the R.B.I. The office has a strange name
— the R.B.I. Ombudsman.
