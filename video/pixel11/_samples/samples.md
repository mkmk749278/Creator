# Pixel 11: Google-published camera samples

Checked 5 Oct 2026. Every item comes from a Google page (blog.google or store.google.com). Full metadata is in `samples.json`.

**Label for every item:** `Source: Google (manufacturer sample, not an independent test)`

**Model:** Google gives no per-image "shot on <model>" credit anywhere, so every item is recorded as *Pixel 11 series (model not stated)*. The JSON notes which product page each image appeared on.

## Count
- **13 stills** (Google's own image files)
- **7 frames** taken from 4 Google demo videos (zoom, Add Me, Best Take)
- **6 source videos** in `video_sources/`

## Gaps
- **No 5x telephoto sample with a stated zoom step.** There are no 5x, 10x or 30x stills from the Pro.
- **No sample is tied to a specific model**, and none has EXIF data. The pages give no lens, mode or settings for any image except the Night Sight and zoom labels.
- No Google still is captioned as motion or action. Magic Capture output exists only inside UI demo videos.
- No Camera Looks "Original vs Look" pair, and no Pro Res Zoom before/after pair as stills.
- Camera Looks images are 1000px at most. The zoom video frames are small (566px and 1392px).
- No Pixel 11 press kit was found. The blog.google image library has no Pixel 11 assets.

## Usage terms
- The blog.google image library says: *"Images on this page may be used for publication with credit: 'Source: Google.'"* It has no Pixel 11 assets, so that grant may not cover these images.
- The blog post and Store pages state no terms. Credit "Source: Google" on screen and confirm with press@google.com before publishing.
