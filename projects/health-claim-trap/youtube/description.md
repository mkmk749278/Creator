WHAT THE VIDEO DID NOT HAVE TIME FOR (all sourced)
- If you do not accept the award, you can still go to a consumer commission.
- File with the Ombudsman within one year of the insurer's final reply (cioins.co.in).
- If you increase your sum insured, the extra amount gets its own five-year count.
- Insurers and TPAs must collect claim documents from the hospital for cashless claims (IRDAI Master Circular, para 17(c)).
- Ombudsman figures: of all health complaints disposed in 2024-25, 53.6% ended in the policyholder's favour; of those settled or decided, 76% (CIO Annual Report 2024-25, Statement H3; our calculation).
- Consumables: estimates vary from 2-8% of a bill (before Covid) to 10-15% (2026 reports); check your own bill.
