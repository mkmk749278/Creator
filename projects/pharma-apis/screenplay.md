# Screenplay: The Pharmacy of the World's Missing Powder

Times are the **raw VO** from `align/align.json` (10:47.2). **+N s** marks a planned picture-led beat (VO stops, the
picture and its own sound carry). The beats add about 18 s, so **Kakinada lands at about 7:55 in the film**, which keeps
the "around minute eight" promise (L12). Re-check against the final cut. Q = the question the viewer is holding
(lean-in test, PLAYBOOK §4a-12). Look: "clean room" (`treatment.md`): pharma white and steel on deep teal; mould green
= penicillin, amber = China's supply, saffron-white = Indian-made, red stamp = a stopped plant. Signature device: the
**CAPSULE GAUGE** (bottom-right, a translucent capsule that fills amber to the share from China, with its source tag).
Kinds: **R** real footage/photo/document, **A** animated HyperFrames scene, **3D** Blender/Three.js, **S** stock.
