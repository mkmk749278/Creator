## Description

```
Every time there is a train crash, our family WhatsApp groups fill with two forwards: "the government never bothered with safety" and "India has Kavach, crashes are impossible now". Both are wrong.

In this video we open the railway's own documents, do the braking maths together, and read what the official inquiry reports found about Balasore (2023) and the Kanchanjunga Express (2024). Kavach is a real, Indian-built safety layer that brakes early when a human slips, as long as the engine is fitted, the track section is working, the signalling data is right and there is room to stop. It is also a chain of four links, and it can only act on the information it is given.

Three things you can do:
1. Before you share a Kavach forward, ask three questions: is the number about fitted equipment or Kavach that is working, which date is it from, and was the problem a human mistake or wrong signalling data?
2. Read the real thing: PIB press releases and the Commission of Railway Safety reports.
3. See something unsafe on a train or at a station? Report it to Rail Madad: call 139 or use the Rail Madad app.

Tell me in the comments: which route do you travel most, and do you know if Kavach is working on it yet?
```
