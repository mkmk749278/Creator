# Research landscape: what everyone says vs what it actually is (2026-11-05)

## 1. What everyone is saying

**News, Oct–Nov 2026.** The DRDO AIP "energy module" entered *Setting to Work Phase-2* at L&T's AM Naik Heavy
Engineering Complex, Hazira, on 24 October 2026; it is to be energised, tested to the Navy's protocols, then handed to
Mazagon Dock for integration on a Kalvari-class submarine in early 2027 (Marine Insight; idrw; several aggregators —
no PIB release found yet). Headlines: "DRDO breakthrough", "turns subs into ghosts", "undetectable".

**The quieter story under the headlines.**
- INS Kalvari's first refit went ahead **without** AIP because the plug was not ready (reported 4 Aug 2025). INS
  Khanderi was chosen instead; targets reported for the fit range from July 2026 to December 2026, now integration
  "early 2027", sea trials 2027, back in service ~2028 (asianet, bharatshakti, idrw; dates conflict).
- **Project 75I** (six new submarines, TKMS design built by Mazagon Dock) uses Germany's **sea-proven fuel-cell AIP**,
  not DRDO's: the tender required an AIP already proven at sea, and DRDO's had only run on land (ORF; Naval Group
  exited in 2022 over the same clause). Contract still awaiting final approval as of the latest reports (≈₹70,000
  crore / ~$8 bn, figures differ).
- **Pakistan's first Hangor-class submarine** (Chinese-built, reported AIP) was commissioned in Sanya in April 2026 and
  reached Karachi on 11 June 2026; eight planned by ~2028 (Dawn, Baird Maritime, Arab News).
- India's conventional fleet is about 16 boats, many old: Kilo (Sindhughosh) and Shishumar classes retiring from the
  late 2020s; INS Sindhughosh decommissioned 19 Dec 2025 (reports; counts differ by source).

**YouTube (channel API search, 2026-11-05).** The biggest pieces are general explainers (Khan GS "Submarine | AIP"
6.5M views; Science Channel "How do submarines dive and surface?" 1.3M; H I Sutton "AIP explained" 210K). India-AIP
videos are short news reads with hype titles ("Ghost", "Undetectable", "DRDO का धमाका"): 25K–70K views. A 34-second
short, "Why the Navy chose proven AIP tech over DRDO's", has 171K views and the angriest comments.

## 2. What it actually is (primary and strong sources; draft claims in `../claims.csv`)
- **Why a diesel boat surfaces:** it runs on batteries under water; the diesels need air, so to recharge it must
  surface or snorkel (mast up), when it is noisiest and visible to radar and eyes (USNI 1990; general references).
  Batteries last only days at slow speed (USNI 1990: 4–10 days without snorkelling, design-dependent).
- **What AIP does, honestly:** weeks of **slow** submerged endurance (example: Type 214 ~60 h at 4 knots on batteries
  vs over a month with AIP — secondary); it is not for speed; sprints still drain the battery in under an hour. It
  shrinks the time spent exposed; it does not make a boat invisible.
- **India's AIP is different from the world's:** a **phosphoric acid fuel cell** fed by hydrogen **made on board** by
  hydrolysing **sodium borohydride** with water (DRDO patent WO2017145068A1; NMRL presentation; Defense News 2023;
  Janes 2021). No stored hydrogen gas. The by-product, sodium metaborate, is dissolved in the spent liquid and kept in
  a tank or discharged (patent). Liquid oxygen is carried for the cell.
- **The land test:** 8 March 2021, Mumbai, land-based prototype run in "endurance mode and max power mode as per the
  user requirements" (MoD statement via Janes / Asian Military Review). ~14 days endurance aim (secondary).
- **The plug:** to fit it, the submarine is **cut in two** and an AIP section inserted: ~8.3 m, ~300 t; length grows
  from 67.56 m to 77.56 m (Naval News 2023; Wikipedia; idrw). DRDO–Naval Group agreement, 23 Jan 2023, for design and
  integration; L&T prime contractor, Thermax fuel cells (Defense News).
- **Kalvari class:** six French Scorpène-design boats built by MDL; 50-day patrol endurance (Janes: 52) (Wikipedia,
  Janes).

## 3. Our edge (one sentence)
**Everyone says AIP will make India's submarines invisible ghosts; the real story is a powder that becomes fuel, a
submarine that must be cut in half to carry it, and a trap of time — a system can't be trusted until it has been to
sea, and it can't go to sea until a submarine is cut open for it.**

Why this is ours: no existing film explains the mechanism one layer down (borohydride → hydrogen → acid fuel cell → the
waste tank), none shows the cut-in-half plug, and none answers the viewers' real anger ("why didn't the Navy take
DRDO's?") honestly, with both sides.
