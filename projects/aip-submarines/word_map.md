# Word map · Why India's Submarines Must Surface (PLAYBOOK §9.5)

Times come from the locked audio (`vo/timing.json`, 541.47 s). `S#` = sentence id. Base shot carries the sentence;
`→ word` = a cutaway or a new element that lands on that word (within 100 ms; cutaways start 0.15 s before). Footage
IDs and spans are in `footage_log.md`. **H** = Blender Cycles hero, **T** = Three.js in Remotion, **R** = Remotion
drawing. Labels on illustrations: ILLUSTRATION / RECONSTRUCTION / OUR CALCULATION.

## Art direction
- **The sea at night** is the film's ground: black-blue water, one cold moon glint, the hull a dark shape under it.
  Every drawing sits on a **steel inspection plate with a drafting grid** (one plate, one tilt, one light for the film).
- **Colour means something:** amber = fuel and hydrogen (the powder's energy); cyan = current, sonar and data; frost
  white-blue = liquid oxygen; **red only for exposure** (the red marks, being found); green = proven / verified.
- **Motif: the patrol strip.** A horizontal strip of time with red marks. It appears at S50, shrinks the marks at
  S54–56, merges them at S62, comes back at S72, and its last red mark goes out at S177 ("the mast stays down").
- **The trap loop** (S141–145): two halves, an arrow that runs between them and closes; it opens at S172 and closes
  again at S180.
- Type: Inter Tight 800 caps for labels (≥ 44 px when the voice points to it), JetBrains Mono for numbers.
