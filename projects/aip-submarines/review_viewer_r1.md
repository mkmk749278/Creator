### Compared with the existing videos

**What this cut does better:**
- **Against the "Ghost" / "Undetectable" news shorts:** it actually explains the mechanism (powder plus water gives hydrogen, oxygen comes from the liquid tank, the fuel cell makes electricity, water loops back) and explicitly says AIP is not a ghost.
- **Against the angry 34-second "proven over DRDO" short:** it gives the fair answer ("both were right", the refit catch-22) without picking a side. It defuses anger instead of feeding it.
- **Against Science Channel:** it's India-specific, has stakes and a story, and uses Indian scale anchors (autorickshaws, a phone charge).
- **Against H I Sutton:** it's far more accessible, with honest "as reported" and "on paper" tags.

**What they do better:**
- **Khan GS:** a live human teacher in Hindi. That builds trust and reaches a mass audience this English narration doesn't. It also repeats key ideas more slowly.
- **H I Sutton:** deeper technical comparison across navies, and visibly expert sourcing.

- **The shorts:** speed. This cut's slow middle (6:00–6:30, 7:26–7:53) would lose their audience.

### Top 8 problems, ranked

1. **8:12–8:13: a borrowed news frame with its own caption.**
   - **What's wrong:** "DESIGNED TO OPERATE IN ALL THEATRES; INTEROPERABLE" has nothing to do with the narration and overlaps the film's own credit tag. It looks lifted, and it hurts trust.
   - **Fix:** Replace it with the clean Khanderi surfacing shot already used at 7:24 and 8:14, or crop the caption out.

2. **4:36–4:44: the Defence Secretary's quote sits on footage of Navy sailors.**
   - **What's wrong:** Viewers read the man on screen as the Defence Secretary.
   - **Fix:** Put the quote on a plain text card with the attribution, or over equipment-only footage (the engine-room shot at 4:44) with no faces.

3. **7:26–7:53: 27 seconds of static map plus a list of places.**
   - **What's wrong:** This is the biggest drift point for impatient and audio-only viewers.
   - **Fix:** Make it a four-step left-to-right timeline (Ambernath lab → Hazira tests → Mazagon fitting → sea trials) with a moving Khanderi icon and bold dates. Merge the "designed / built / fitted" credits into that same card instead of repeating the map.

   - **Fix:** Crop to a full 16:9 frame, or use a horizontal P-8I shot. Failing that, animate a P-8I silhouette sweeping over the sea texture used at 1:26.
