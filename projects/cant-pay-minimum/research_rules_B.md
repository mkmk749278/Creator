## B1. Recovery agent conduct rules in force in 2026

**Key timing note (new, important):** RBI issued FINAL new recovery directions on 6 Aug 2026 that take effect **1 Jan 2027**. So in Oct 2026 the rules in force are still the 2008 / 2022 circulars plus the Credit Card Master Direction 2022. The 8 am to 7 pm window and the ban on harassing relatives exist in BOTH the current and the new rules, so the core message is safe either way.

**B1.1 No calls before 8 am or after 7 pm (recovery calls).** TRUE.
- RBI circular RBI/2022-23/108, DOR.ORG.REC.65/21.04.158/2022-23, 12 Aug 2022, "Outsourcing of Financial Services - Responsibilities of regulated entities employing Recovery Agents", para 2: prohibits "Calling the borrower before 8:00 a.m. and after 7:00 p.m. for recovery of overdue loans". https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/FS_Notification.aspx?Id=12378
- Same circular para 2 also bars "intimidation or harassment of any kind", "threatening and/or anonymous calls", "persistent calling", "inappropriate messages either on mobile or through social media", "false and misleading representations", and intruding on the privacy of the debtor's "family members, referees and friends". Para 4: violations "will be viewed seriously". Para 5: applies to commercial banks (excluding payments banks), AIFIs, NBFCs incl. HFCs, cooperative banks, ARCs.
- New rule from 1 Jan 2027: RBI/2026-27/223, DOR.MCS.REC.No.193/01-01-032/2026-27, 6 Aug 2026, "(Commercial Banks - Responsible Business Conduct) Fourth Amendment Directions, 2026", para 454Y(4): "An employee / recovery agent shall contact / visit the borrower / guarantor only between 08:00 hours and 19:00 hours." (outside only on borrower's express request). Effective date para 3(2): 1 Jan 2027. https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=13665
- Note: Credit Card MD 2022 para 6(a)(ix) is a separate, narrower window for marketing contact: "The card-issuer's representatives shall contact the customers only between 10:00 hrs and 19:00 hrs." (this is the telemarketing/DSA clause, not recovery). Do not mix the two.

**B1.2 No harassing or humiliating relatives, friends, colleagues; no threats; privacy.** TRUE.
- Credit Card MD 2022 (RBI/2022-23/92, 21 Apr 2022, updated 7 Mar 2024), para 13(c): bars "intimidation or harassment of any kind, either verbal or physical", threatening or anonymous calls, public humiliation, intruding on the privacy of family, referees or friends, false representations. Para 13(a): card-issuers and agents follow the Fair Practices Code when recovering. Para 13(e): only information needed for recovery may be shared with agents. https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12300
- From 2027 (RBI/2026-27/223): para 454Z(6) bans "Intimidating or harassing the borrower / guarantor and / or his / her relatives, referees, friends, or co-workers", "intruding upon their privacy" and "acts intended to humiliate them publicly"; 454Z(5) "Making threatening and / or anonymous calls;"; 454Z(1) "Use of minatory or abusive language;". Para 454U: bank/tech provider may not access phone "contacts, SMS, call logs, photos, location history, etc." (Same URL as above.)
- "Colleagues/co-workers" is named explicitly only in the 2027 rule. For 2026, say "family, friends, referees" (2022 wording) or "people you know".

**B1.3 Agent must carry ID card, bank's authorisation letter and copy of bank's notice; bank must tell you the agency's details.** TRUE.
- RBI circular RBI/2007-2008/296, DBOD.No.Leg.BC.75/09.07.005/2007-08, 24 Apr 2008, "Recovery Agents engaged by banks", para 2(iii): banks "should inform the borrower the details of recovery agency firms / companies while forwarding default cases"; the agent "carries a copy of the notice and the authorization letter from the bank" "along with the identity card issued to him by the bank or the agency firm / company". Para 2(iv): "tape recording of the content / text of the calls made by recovery agents". https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=4141
- Credit Card MD 2022 para 13(b): card-issuer gives the cardholder the agent's name and contact details "immediately upon assigning the agent to the cardholder" (URL above).
- From 2027 (RBI/2026-27/223) para 454X: agents "identify themselves by displaying their identity card issued by the bank and recovery agency", carry "an authorisation letter issued by the bank or the recovery agency" and "a copy of the notice issued by the bank in terms of paragraph 454L"; para 454L: agency details to borrower at least one day before the first in-person visit.

**B1.4 The bank is responsible for its agents.** TRUE.
- 2008 circular para 3: "Banks, as principals, are responsible for the actions of their agents." Para 4: RBI "may consider imposing a ban on a bank from engaging recovery agents in a particular area" for abusive practices. (URL Id=4141)
- 2022 circular: REs remain responsible for the actions of service providers incl. recovery agents (Id=12378).
- 2027 rule para 454V: bank must "put in place a management structure to monitor and control the activities of its recovery agencies"; 454AA: "A bank shall have a dedicated mechanism for redressal of recovery related grievances."

**B1.5 Credit Card MD 2022 still the live card rule? / RBI/DOR/2025-26/155.** UNVERIFIED. The RBI MD page (id=12300) opened 2026-10-12 is not marked repealed. I could not open the 2025 consolidated credit card direction (RBI/DOR/2025-26/155) text in time; its recovery paragraph number is not confirmed. Cite the 2022 MD para 13 or the 2022 circular instead.

## B2. Is credit card default a crime?

**B2.1 Not paying a loan, by itself, is not cheating. Cheating needs a dishonest intention right at the start.** TRUE (Supreme Court).
- *Satishchandra Ratanlal Shah v. State of Gujarat*, Criminal Appeal No. 9 of 2019, Supreme Court (N.V. Ramana and Mohan M. Shantanagoudar, JJ.), judgment 3 Jan 2019, para 14: "The mere inability of the appellant to return the loan amount cannot give rise to a criminal prosecution for cheating unless fraudulent or dishonest intention is shown right at the beginning of the transaction, as it is this mens rea which is the crux of the offence." Para 15: the Court has "cautioned against criminalizing civil disputes, such as breach of contractual obligations". FIR quashed (para 18). https://api.sci.gov.in/supremecourt/2018/17145/17145_2018_Judgement_03-Jan-2019.pdf (PDF opened and text extracted)
- Context: a ~Rs 27 lakh private loan, not a credit card; the principle is general (IPC 415/420, now BNS 318). Note: the 2019 ruling is under IPC; BNS 318 (in force 1 Jul 2024) is the successor cheating section (not separately opened here).

**B2.2 Police / FIRs are not a way to recover money in a civil dispute.** TRUE (Supreme Court 2025).
- *Shailesh Kumar v. State of Uttar Pradesh* (Pardiwala and Mahadevan, JJ.), reported by Verdictum 20 Jul 2025: "Money cannot be recovered, more particularly, in a civil dispute between the parties by filing a First Information Report" ... "This amounts to abuse of process of law." https://www.verdictum.in/app-lite/court-updates/supreme-court/shailesh-kumar-vs-state-of-uttar-pradesh-recover-money-civil-dispute-fir-1585416
- *Shrichand Rajaram Kukreja v. State of Maharashtra*, 2025 INSC 794, para 18 (via CaseCiter, 2 Jun 2025): complainant tried to "involve the police machinery to act as recovery agents on his behalf"; "The allegations, made in the complaint, present a dispute which is purely commercial and civil in nature." https://www.caseciter.com/shrichand-rajaram-kukreja-vs-state-of-maharashtra-2025-insc-794-quashing-civil-disputes-criminal-cases/
- Neither case is about a credit card. No credit-card-specific HC ruling was opened in this session (UNVERIFIED for card-specific case law). The principle still applies to card dues, which are an unsecured loan.

**B2.3 Police can arrest you just for card dues.** FALSE in law as a general statement, but say it carefully: "not paying alone is not a crime, so there is nothing to arrest you for". No single ruling saying "police cannot arrest for card dues" was opened; the SC lines above (B2.1, B2.2) are the basis. PARTLY sourced.

**B2.4 What CAN become criminal.** TRUE.
- **Cheque bounce** (Section 138 NI Act) and **failed ECS/NACH/auto-debit for lack of funds** (Section 25 PSS Act 2007). RBI circular DPSS.CO.PD.No.497/02.12.004/2011-12, 21 Sep 2011, "Dishonour of electronic funds transfer for insufficiency of funds in the bank account - clarification": "section 25 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 accords the same rights and remedies to the payee" as section 138 NI Act; sub-section (5) provides "punishment of two years and twice the amount of electronic funds transfer instruction, or both." https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_CircularIndexDisplay.aspx?Id=6723
- Caveat: these need the statutory notice steps (written demand within 30 days, 15 days to pay; per law-firm summary bnblegal.com, not opened). Card dues paid without a cheque/mandate do not trigger them. Practice for card dues: see research_practical.md 3.6 (UNVERIFIED there).
- **Fraud at application** (fake documents, intent never to pay from day one) fits the "dishonest intention at the beginning" test in B2.1, so it can be cheating. Inference from B2.1, PARTLY.

## B3. Fake or scary "legal notices" vs real court summons

**B3.1 A lawyer's legal notice is not a court summons. A summons is issued by a court, after a case is filed.** TRUE.
- Statute: BNSS 2023 Section 63: "Every summons issued by a Court under this Sanhita shall be" in writing, signed by the presiding officer (courtbook.in reproduction of the Act, opened 2026-10-12). https://courtbook.in/bnss/section-63
- Explainer (law-firm blog, no date, (c) 2026): "A legal notice is a written caution that an advocate draughts on the client's behalf." / "A court notice is sent directly from the court to the opposing party." Ignoring a legal notice: "refusing to comply with it won't hurt you too much", but the sender may then file a case. https://www.leadindia.law/blog/en/the-difference-between-a-lawyer-notice-and-a-court-notice/
- Practical framing: a legal notice is the bank saying "we may sue". It is worth reading and answering, not ignoring, because the next step can be a real case (Section 138/25 cases need a demand notice first, see B2.4).

**B3.2 "A real summons never comes on WhatsApp / by email."** FALSE as an absolute. DO NOT SAY.
- BNSS Section 63(ii): a summons may be issued "in an encrypted or any other form of electronic communication" and "shall bear the image of the seal of the Court or digital signature." https://courtbook.in/bnss/section-63
- Supreme Court, *Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI*, 2025 INSC 909 (via Verdictum headline/summary, search result, not opened): summons under 63(i) or 63(ii) must carry the court's seal or its image; electronic service is not allowed for a Section 35 police notice. Bombay HC 2018 accepted a bank's notice served on WhatsApp (thebetterindia/businesstoday 2018, search results, not opened).
- Amar Ujala, 16 May 2026 (Hindi): "आमतौर पर कोई भी अदालत या थाना आपको सीधे व्हाट्सएप पर समन नहीं भेजता है।" (Generally no court or police station sends you a summons directly on WhatsApp.) Advises going to the named police station to verify; report fraud to 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in. https://www.amarujala.com/photo-gallery/utility/is-whatsapp-police-summon-legally-valid-cyber-fraud-alert-and-new-safety-guidelines-2026-05-16
- Safe line: "Most real summons come by post or in person. If one comes on your phone, it must carry the court's seal and a case number you can check yourself."

**B3.3 Lenders send legal notices in bulk, automatically, by SMS/email/WhatsApp.** TRUE (vendor's own claim; volume figures UNVERIFIED).
- Provakil (legal-tech vendor) product page, opened 2026-10-12: "Generate legally compliant, multilingual notices in bulk, directly from borrower and case data"; sent "via SMS, email, and WhatsApp"; FAQ: banks and NBFCs "generate and deliver millions of notices per month." https://provakil.com/legal-collections-management/notice-automation
- See also research_practical.md 1.10 (BOBCARD tender for digital legal notices). So a scary notice on your phone is often a mass-produced template, not a case filed against you.

**B3.4 Fake court / police / arrest notices are a known scam.** TRUE (search summaries; pages not individually opened, so quote only after opening).
- Search results 2026-10-12: Mumbai Police Commissioner warning on fake "arrest notices" (mypunepulse.com), Bombay HC warning on fraud messages in judges' names (deccanherald.com, 2024), Supreme Court suo motu on forged court orders in "digital arrest" scams (moneylife.in). UNVERIFIED until opened.

**B3.5 How to check a real summons: eCourts CNR number.** TRUE.
- eCourts Services portal (e-Committee, Supreme Court of India; NIC), opened 2026-10-12: CNR is a 16-character code entered without hyphens or spaces, example "MHAU019999992015"; if you do not know the CNR, case status can be searched by "case registration number, party name, advocate name etc." https://services.ecourts.gov.in/ecourtindia_v6/
- So: take the CNR or case number from the paper, type it yourself on the official site or eCourts app; never click the link in the message.
