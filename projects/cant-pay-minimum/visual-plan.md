# Can't Pay Even the Minimum: visual plan (v1, 2026-10-13)

The VO is the owner's recording (Akash, eleven_v4, 3 blocks, 11:22). Times come from `build/timing.py` and `build/words.py`
(word-level, no Whisper). Each line has a picture that shows what is said at that second (semantic lock), with a cut,
page turn or new item every 2.5–4 s.

## Look (owner, 2026-10-12/13)
- **DESK** (big moments): a photoreal night desk on walnut under a warm lamp. Real-looking props (card, bill, phone,
  notices) come from the props studio (`video/lib/props`), composited as 2.5D plates with a moving camera, a buzzing phone,
  screen glow, dust and DOF. Words appear only on the objects, plus a lower-third.
- **PAGE** (explaining): the doodle kit on paper with **photoreal props** ("use ultra realistic photos for card, bill,
  etc.", owner 2026-10-13). The card, bill, phone, notice and cheque are photo cut-outs with real shadows. The ink
  (rough circles, arrows, stamps, short typed labels, numbers) is drawn on and around them. No doodled cards or bills
  any more.
- **FOOTAGE**: real Indian footage first, stock second. "ILLUSTRATIVE FOOTAGE" is tagged where a stand-in could pass as
  the real event.
- **PROOF**: the real source page (RBI, bank MITC, judgment) with the quoted words highlighted and a citation tag.
- Every bill, card and phone screen is ours: fictional names (Ravi, Sunita), no real bank logos, a `SIMULATION` tag.
- Music: tension bed (acts 1–3), **Dreamer** (Incompetech, CC BY) from the five steps (C071), Promises to Keep under the
  end card. There is 1.5 s of silence before the reveals at C013 (NPA), C027 (Ravi's 19,000) and C064 (not a crime),
  then a low thud.

Shot kinds: `D` desk, `P` page, `F` footage, `R` proof, `T` title/sting/end.
