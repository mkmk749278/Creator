## Description (copy everything between the lines)

---
Your credit card bill says Total ₹30,000, Minimum ₹1,500. Is paying only the minimum safe? It saves you from a late
fee and a bad CIBIL report, but not from interest. In this video we work out the full maths, in simple words: the
interest free period, the one condition that keeps it, how the bank charges interest from the day you spent, why you
pay interest even on the money you already paid, 18% GST on top, and what happens if you pay only the minimum for
years. We use ICICI Bank's own published example, the RBI's credit card rules, and our own worked example.

5 simple steps at the end: pay the full bill (set auto pay to the full amount), pay as much as you can, use UPI or a
debit card till the bill is cleared, ask your bank for an EMI instead of the minimum, and move your bill date after
your salary.

What do you pay every month, the full bill or the minimum? Tell me in the comments.
