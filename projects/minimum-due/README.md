# Minimum Amount Due: the trap behind the smallest number on your credit card bill (English explainer, Telugu dub text)

Telugu explainer for *Be Practical with Kishore* on the credit card **Minimum Amount Due (MAD)**: what MAD really protects
(late fee, "past due" credit reporting) and what it doesn't (interest); the interest-free period and its one condition;
the four rules the bank's system applies once you pay less than the total (interest from each spend date, interest on
the part you paid until the day you paid it, 18% GST, no free days on new spends); a worked ₹30,000 example in which
paying the ₹1,500 minimum leaves you owing **more** (₹30,481); ICICI Bank's own published statement example (₹65,000
of ₹69,688 paid, ₹4,317 interest + GST); what paying only the minimum costs over 1 and 5 years; the system (11.86 crore
cards, ₹2.98 lakh crore outstanding, CRIF overdue data, Ombudsman complaints, the 2008 NCDRC cap and its 2024 Supreme
Court reversal); why card rotation only postpones the debt; and six practical steps. Script written by Claude from the
owner's brief and reference spec (2026-10-11; the spec was used as a reference only). VO to be generated by the owner on
ElevenLabs (Bunty, `eleven_v4`). Planned as part 1 of a credit-card series; part 2 = "when you can't pay even the
minimum".

## Research record (writer's pass, 2026-10-11; the fact-check re-verifies independently)
- **RBI rules.** RBI (Commercial Banks – Credit Cards and Debit Cards: Issuance and Conduct) Directions, 2025
  (RBI/DOR/2025-26/155, 28 Nov 2025, updated 1 Oct 2026; repeals the 2022 Master Direction, para 93), via the text
  reproduced on [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/reserve-bank-india-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-directions-2025.html):
  MAD definition 5(17); interest-free credit period 5(16); no negative amortisation and no capitalisation of unpaid
  charges 23(2); interest-free period suspended + statement warning 23(3); late charges and "past due" reporting only
  after more than three days 23(5); interest-free period lost, interest from the transaction date, interest only on the
  outstanding adjusted for payments 23(6). The official PDF (`rbidocs.rbi.org.in/rdocs/notification/PDFs/155MD.PDF`) sits
  behind a captcha from the cloud; the 2022 Master Direction text ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12300))
  has the same clauses (9(b)(ii)–(vi), Annex ₹10,000 / 2% / ₹250 MAD illustration, 10(f) billing-cycle change).
  Amendment Directions 2026 (27 Apr 2026, effective 1 Apr 2027) restate 23(5) ([TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/rbi/rbi-commercial-banks-credit-cards-debit-cards-issuance-conduct-amendment-directions-2026.html)).
- **ICICI Bank worked example** ([icici.bank.in interest-rates page](https://www.icici.bank.in/personal-banking/blogs/card/credit-card/interest-rates),
  accurate as of 30 Sep 2025): 3.75% a month = 45% a year; statement 12/09/2025, TAD ₹69,688, MAD ₹3,490; payment
  ₹65,000 on 30/09/2025 (the due date); statement 12/10/2025 interest ₹3,658.65 + GST ₹658.56 = ₹4,317.21; formula
  Interest = Days/365 × Amount × Rate. Page inconsistencies (text says ₹3,414.74; TAD 46,679.89 vs closing 46,967.71) are
  not used.
- **HDFC Bank MAD formula** from the June 2025 statement ([HDFC notice](https://v.hdfc.bank.in/htdocs/common/mad-communication.html)):
  GST + EMI + 100% fees + 5% of (finance charge + retail + cash), or 100% of the finance charge + 5% of (retail + cash)
  when that is larger. HDFC MITC: a part payment makes the whole outstanding and all new transactions attract finance
  charges until the previous outstanding is repaid in full. SmartEMI page: IRR ≈ 11.88–24.84% (Apr–Jun 2025), ₹849 + GST
  processing fee.
- **Our calculations** (`tools/mad_math.py`; ICICI method with the transaction day counted, HDFC MAD formula with the 5%
  part rounded up to ₹10, 45% p.a., 18% GST, payments to GST then interest then principal, no interest on GST): spends
  5/12/20 Oct ₹10k/₹15k/₹5k, statement 25 Oct, due 14 Nov, MAD ₹1,500 paid on 14 Nov, statement 25 Nov → interest
  ₹1,679.18 + GST ₹302.25 = ₹1,981.43; balance ₹30,481.43. With ₹5,000 petrol on 15 Nov: total ₹35,561.44, MAD ₹2,084.46,
  that month's interest + GST ₹2,061.44. Paying ₹29,999 instead: interest + GST ₹1,483.91. Paying the MAD instead of
  nothing saves only ₹22 of interest. MAD-only, no new spends (the ₹1,500 is payment 1): after 12 payments paid ₹19,987,
  owe ₹24,771 (about 74% of the money paid went to interest + GST); after 60 payments paid ₹69,896, owe ₹12,956; about
  15.7 years to clear at a ₹200 floor (the film says "more than ten years").
- **Scale.** 118.63 million cards in force, end-March 2026 (RBI data via [Mint, 1 May 2026](https://www.livemintmoney.com/personal-finance/credit-cards/total-number-of-credit-cards-hit-over-118-million-in-march-2026-shows-rbi-data-281777631894672.html));
  credit card outstanding ₹2,97,756 crore (RBI Bulletin, Sep 2026, Current Statistics table 4.5; the column's reference
  date must be confirmed); ₹33,886 crore 91–360 days overdue, +44% YoY, March 2025 (CRIF High Mark via
  [Angel One, 30 Jul 2025](https://www.angelone.in/news/personal-finance/indians-struggle-to-repay-credit-card-dues-as-overdue-bills-soar-44-percent-to-rs-33886-crore));
  credit cards 17.15% (≈50,811) of RBI Ombudsman complaints, FY 2024–25, second after loans and advances (RBI Annual
  Report of the Ombudsman Scheme 2024–25, via [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/12/223-rbi-ombudsman-14-banking-complaints-fy25-private-banks/));
  AP 43.7% / Telangana 37.2% of adults in debt (MoSPI 2020–21, PLAYBOOK §4a).
- **Courts.** NCDRC, *Awaz v. RBI*, 7 Jul 2008: above 30% a year is an unfair trade practice; set aside by the Supreme
  Court on 20 Dec 2024, *HSBC v. Awaz*, 2024 INSC 1044 (Trivedi and Sharma JJ.): encroaches on the RBI's domain;
  customers are told the MITC including the rate ([Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/news/supreme-court-sets-aside-ncdrc-judgment-capping-interest-credit-card-dues-30),
  [Verdictum](https://www.verdictum.in/court-updates/supreme-court/hongkong-and-shanghai-banking-corp-ltd-v-awaz-2024-insc-1044-ncdrc-declaring-credit-card-interest-rates-beyond-30-set-aside-1561914)).
- **Rent via apps.** RBI (Regulation of Payment Aggregators) Directions, 15 Sep 2025; PhonePe, Paytm and CRED removed
  credit-card rent payments ([Business Standard, 18 Sep 2025](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/phonepe-paytm-cred-end-rent-service-as-rbi-tightens-payment-rules-125091800422_1.html),
  [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2025/09/223-rbi-pa-rules-phonepe-paytm-cred-credit-card-rent-payments/)).
- **Reference spec, corrected.** The owner's reference doc put month-2 MAD at "₹6,500+" and the grace period at
  "40–45 days" for every spend; the real formulas give ≈₹2,070 for that scenario, and free days depend on the spend date
  (40 and 25 days in our example). Its "Section 171 salary sweep" teaser is not used (unverified); the part-2 teaser
