## Description

```
Your cashless claim was denied, you paid the hospital yourself and claimed it back... and the insurance company rejected it again, or cut most of it. Before you hire a lawyer or sign up with a claim-recovery agent who takes 20% of your money, know the third door: the Insurance Ombudsman. Filing costs zero rupees and you can do it yourself online.

In this video (part 2 of our health insurance series): why Bima Bharosa cannot decide your case, the one common mistake that gets complaints turned away, the two-step clock under Rule 14, how to file on cioins.co.in step by step, what happens at the hearing, and what the insurance company must do after it loses.

What to do:
1. Write to the insurance company first, best of all to its Grievance Redressal Officer, and keep every email.
2. If it rejects you, you are not happy with the reply, or one month passes with no reply, you can go to the Ombudsman. You have one year from then, for claims up to 50 lakh rupees.
3. File free at https://cioins.co.in (Register Complaint). Never share passwords or OTPs with anyone.

Drop your questions below. If you missed part 1, watch it first: what to do when cashless is denied at the hospital.
```
