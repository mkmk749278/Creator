- Some Mixkit hospital clips were found under a personal-use-only restricted licence; none was included as production stock.
- News/documentary footage was not downloaded as if it were free stock.
- An initially retrieved cash-counting shot showed old-series ₹500 notes. It was removed and replaced with a current-series ₹100 counting-machine candidate under F17. It is not included in the final pack.
- No verified original Hyderabad hospital interior, genuine denied-claim family interview or actual Ombudsman hearing was obtained. Generic substitutes are labelled in the register.
- No current geographic India boundary map was included. G08 is an office-name board, not a political map.
- No AI-generated raster imagery is included. Graphics are original vector/data-layout work.

## Verification performed

All included stock MP4s were opened with ffprobe. Three time samples from every clip were extracted and visually reviewed. The rules PDF and annual report were opened; selected pages were rendered. Every provided graphic was rendered and inspected for legibility. This is sample-based review, not a frame-by-frame rights or content audit.

Keep original source URLs with the project and revisit the relevant licence/current procedural source at publication if anything changes.
