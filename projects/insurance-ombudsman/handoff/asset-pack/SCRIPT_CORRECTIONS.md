# Corrections that affect visual production

This is a targeted production check, not a complete legal opinion or a full audit of later amendments. The supplied script labels its anchors “audited”, but several claims exceed the evidence retrieved. Keep unverified claims out of final narration and graphics until resolved.

## What the source material supports

- The amended rules provide a ₹50 lakh compensation ceiling including relevant expenses, subject to their loss-related limit. Source: Rules PDF p11.
- A prior representation and the relevant response/wait condition matter. Other proceedings pending **or disposed of** on the same subject, including arbitration, can bar this route. Source: Rules PDF p9.
- Free filing and no need to engage a lawyer are described in CIO guidance. Source: CIO FAQ.
- The daily penalty is described by IRDAI Health Department FAQ 45. Source snapshot included.
