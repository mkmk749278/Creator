## Visual approach

Build the film around a believable household and its documents. Use the Hyderabad footage to establish India, then close views of hands, bills, phone screens and keyboard actions. Generic hospital stock should be brief and incidental. Avoid changing actors every sentence as though all are one family.

Use the provided navy, yellow, white and teal graphics as layered assets. Reveal each number, document or process stage separately. A static PNG is a layout reference or fallback, not the intended long-running shot. Follow an approximate 3-second rhythm for the opening; allow 6–10 seconds for a source document or a readable portal explanation. Do not force a hard cut every three seconds during instructions.

Keep synthetic email, bill and call screens clearly labelled **Illustrative example**. Use invented policy and claim numbers, no real Aadhaar, phone number, bank account or medical record. A designed screen must not be passed off as a captured government portal.
