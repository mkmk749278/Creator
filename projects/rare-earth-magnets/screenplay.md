# Screenplay v1: India Has the Rare Earths. China Has the Magnets. Why?

Series "India's Missing Pieces", episode 2 (episode 1 = The $10 Billion Chip Gamble). English VO (`vo.txt`, Akash).
Times are estimates at about 160 words a minute (Akash runs about 10% faster than `estimate_vo.py`); re-time after the
VO is recorded. Line ids = rows of `vo.txt` / `script.tsv`. Every number on screen comes from `claims.csv`.

**Look:** genre = tech + business (PLAYBOOK §5.3), built on the dark blueprint palette (`blueprint` in
`config/brand.yaml`): amber `#d29922` = China's control / cost, cyan `#58a6ff` = magnetism / India's build-out,
green `#3fb950` = a link India now owns, red `#f85149` = a stopped line only. Real footage and photos carry the
story (factories, beaches, plants, documents); 3D carries the mechanism.

**Signature device: THE CHAIN BAR.** A six-link chain across the bottom third, introduced at L29 and back at every
chapter: SAND → MIXED → CLEAN → METAL → ALLOY → MAGNET. Each link lights in amber when China holds it and cyan/green
when India is building it. It's the one picture the viewer should remember (owner: "best visuals", narration simple).

**Persistent HUD (top-left, small):** `IMPORTS 2024-25 · 53,700 T · ~9/10 CHINA` → later `CHINA: 94% OF MAGNETS` →
`HOLD ENDS · 10 NOV 2026` countdown. Values only from `claims.csv`, else "ILLUSTRATIVE".

**Hero 3D shots (Blender Cycles on Kaggle GPU, or Three.js):** (1) scooter hub motor opening to show its magnet ring;
(2) the solvent-extraction cascade: a long row of tanks with the liquid's colour shifting tank by tank;
(3) the magnet-making line: flakes → hydrogen crack → powder → particles snapping into line in a magnetic field →
press → furnace glow → field lines switching on; (4) a monazite grain cut open: rare earths in cyan, thorium glowing.
