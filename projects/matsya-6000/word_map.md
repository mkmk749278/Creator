# Word map · ISRO's Hardest Weld Isn't for a Rocket (PLAYBOOK §9.5)

Times come from the locked audio r2 (`vo/timing.json`, 452.13 s, approved 2026-11-10). `S#` = sentence id. The base
shot carries the sentence; `→ word` = a cutaway or a new element that lands on that word (within 100 ms; cutaways start
0.15 s before). **H** = Blender hero clip in Drive `renders/` (placed so its frame 0 is the given sentence start),
**T** = Three.js in Remotion, **R** = Remotion drawing, **ISRO m:ss** = ISRO's Samudrayaan video (credit "ISRO"),
**F0x** = news clip spans from `media_manifest.csv`, **PIB / IFR** = photos. Labels: ILLUSTRATION on every H and R that
shows Matsya or its parts; SIMPLIFIED on the squeeze; OUR CALCULATION on 7.1 m and "just over two metres".

## Art direction
- **Two worlds, one ball.** The workshop (bare titanium on the steel inspection plate, cool studio light, the film's
  physical base) and the deep (black water, marine snow, the sub's own lamps). Every cut between them goes through
  the ball.
- **Colour means something:** amber = pressure and squeeze; white-hot to orange = a weld being laid; cyan = inspection
  (X-ray, sound); green = certified / verified; **red only for Titan's failure** (and the ball's real red rims).
- **Motif: the seam.** A thin glowing line. It appears in the cold open (S4 "seven welds"), traces in H2_pieces (S62–64),
  is laid in H2_seam (S79–82), and returns as the last image's light (S140 "checked at once").
- Type: Inter Tight 800 caps for labels (≥ 44 px when the voice points to it), JetBrains Mono for numbers.
- **Titan with dignity:** no implosion, no victims, no sound effect. The report's own cover and words on a calm frame.
