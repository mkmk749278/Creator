# Digital Arrest: the scam that jails you in your own home (Telugu explainer)

Telugu explainer for *Be Practical with Kishore* on "digital arrest" cyber scams in India: the scale (I4C complaint data),
the Hyderabad retired district judge who lost ₹1.66 crore (Feb–Mar 2026), the gangs' three-stage playbook (fear →
isolation → money, with the Vardhman/S P Oswal fake Supreme Court hearing and the Haryana suo motu case), the legal reality
(no "digital arrest" in law; BNSS ss. 36, 43, 48, 58 and *D.K. Basu*), a three-step action plan (cut the call · no money,
OTP or apps · bank + 1930 + cybercrime.gov.in + evidence + Chakshu) and the family rule. Script written by Claude from the
owner's brief (2026-10-09); VO to be generated by the owner on ElevenLabs (voice Bunty). Estimated speech ≈ 11:20.

**Status:** phase 1. Script + fact-check done; waiting for the owner's script approval and the Bunty VO. No media,
EDL or render yet.

## Genre and look (PLAYBOOK §5.3, own art direction)
**Tech & digital × society.** Art direction "**interrogation light**": matte black frames lit by a single sodium-amber key
(`#ffb547`), alarm red (`#e5383b`) for every scam artefact (fake warrants, payee names, the REC dot), and grain + 30%
vignette. From the legal reveal (L61) the palette turns to **daylight teal** (`#2ec4b6`) and warm white: the film moves
from the scammers' dark room into the open. Motifs: the red **REC ●** corner timer that keeps counting through Acts 1–2
and is finally switched off; the **glass box** around the isolated victim that cracks (L52) and shatters (L93); the
mule-account **network** that drains (L58) and is frozen (L83).

Media stack: real Hyderabad/Ludhiana/Ambala establishing stock, Indian police station and court exteriors (stock/Commons),
credited news headlines (short excerpts), the official I4C advisory graphic and Mann Ki Baat excerpt (government, credited),
screen recordings of cybercrime.gov.in and Sanchar Saathi; **SIMULATION** HyperFrames scenes for the video-call UI, banking
app, fake warrants and fake courtroom (faces always blurred; never a real emblem, never a real officer's or judge's photo);
2.5D isometric scam-centre model; SVG map/data animation for the I4C numbers and the money network.
YouTube "altered content" = **Yes** (realistic simulations of a real scam type).
