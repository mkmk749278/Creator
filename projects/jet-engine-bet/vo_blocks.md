```
Let's start with the engine. Think of it as a blowtorch that never stops... and runs its own air pump. Strange, right?

Four jobs. Suck. Squeeze. Burn. Blow.

Suck. A giant fan at the front gulps in air. Huge amounts of it.

Squeeze. You've pumped a bicycle tyre, haven't you? You felt the pump get hot. Squeeze air, and it heats up and packs tight. Inside the engine, rows of small fans, called the compressor, do that again and again... until the air is very hot, and very tight.

Burn. Fuel sprays into that air and catches fire. Think of the flame on your gas stove. Now picture it far, far bigger, and it never goes out.

Blow. The hot gas rushes out, and on its way it hits another set of fans, the turbine. It spins them, like wind spinning a ceiling fan.

And here's the clever part. A shaft joins that turbine to the compressor at the front. So the fire spins the turbine, the turbine spins the compressor, and the engine keeps itself alive. The rest of the gas shoots out of the back, and the jet is pushed forward... like a balloon you let go of.
```
