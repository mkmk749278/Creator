# Screenplay: Can India Build Its Own ChatGPT?

Times are the **approved raw VO** (`align/align.json`, 12:52.7, owner approved 2026-11-03). **+N s** = a planned
picture-led beat (the voice stops; picture and its own sound carry). The beats add about 17 s, so the payoff (L68)
lands at about **9:58 in the film**, which keeps the "around minute ten" promise (L18). Re-check in every cut.
Look: "the reading room" (`treatment.md`): library amber and paper against the cold blue-black of a data hall.
Colour = meaning: paper amber = words, chip green = compute, saffron = Indian-built, grey-blue = built abroad,
red cut = a letter split in half. Signature device: the **PIECE METER** (top left: one glowing tile per token, the
language and the chopper named, e.g. `DEEPSEEK CHOPPER · HINDI · 18`). Kinds: **R** real footage/photo/document,
**A** HyperFrames animation, **S** stock (Pexels/Pixabay, `ILLUSTRATIVE FOOTAGE` where it could pass as the event),
**O** owner's Drive footage (`asset-manifest.md`). Q = the question the viewer is holding (§4a-12).
Every number on screen has its claims row; citation tags bottom right above the logo bug.
