# Shot-by-shot storyboards: Acts 1–3 + Outro

**Timecodes are estimates** at the hook's pace (≈2.4 words/s, plus pauses). After you generate each act's VO, drop it on the
timeline, mark the `[pause]` beats and punchlines on the waveform, and slide the cut points to match. The cut order and cues stay the same.

**Asset ID prefixes** (full lists at the end of each act):
`M` map/geo zoom · `D` document/clipping 2.5D · `B` b-roll (licensed stock) · `G` motion graphic built in-house ·
`P` press/official image (press kit or government release, credited) · `S` sound design

**Global sound rules (from the handoff):** 808 sub-boom on punchlines, whoosh on whip pans and map zooms, paper-slide on
document pop-ups, and the ambient score ducked to −18 to −22 dB under speech. Let the score swell to about −12 dB only in
pauses longer than 1 s and on act cards.
