# Fact-check sheet: verify before you generate the VO

Every ⚑ beat in `01-script.md` is listed here. **Status** says how sure we are:
- **Solid:** widely reported; confirm with the primary source linked in the "verify with" column.
- **Check:** the direction is right but the exact number or wording needs a source.
- **Recent:** after mid-2026 or fast-moving; confirm the current status on the day you voice it.

Use primary sources (PIB, company newsrooms, ministry data) over blogs. Write the final source URL in the last column.
It goes into the video description and the credits.
