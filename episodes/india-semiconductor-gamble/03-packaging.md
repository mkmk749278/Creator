## Thumbnail concepts

All use licensed stock or press imagery plus in-house graphics. No AI-generated photos of real plants or products.
Text is 3–4 words, heavy condensed sans in brand colours, readable at 120 px wide (check it on a phone).

### A · "The Gamble" (pairs with title 1)
- **Visual:** A single silicon wafer held up against a dark background, catching saffron/teal rainbow light. Behind it, an India map outline glows faintly.
  On the right, a poker chip stamped **$10B** sits on the wafer edge.
- **Text:** **$10B CHIP GAMBLE**
- **Why it works:** one clear object, one number, high contrast. "Gamble" signals stakes.

### B · "Designed Here, Made There" (pairs with title 2)
- **Visual:** Split frame. Left: India flag colours, chip blueprint lines, labelled ✅. Right: a red-tinted Taiwan map silhouette with a cargo ship, labelled ❌ (or reversed with a 🇮🇳 → 🇹🇼 arrow).
- **Text:** **WE DESIGN IT. THEY MAKE IT.** (or shorter: **DESIGNED, NOT MADE**)
- **Why it works:** a contrast story you can read in half a second.

### C · "Stranded Cars" (pairs with title 4)
- **Visual:** A drone shot of a parking lot packed with new cars, desaturated. One tiny glowing chip in the foreground with a red circle and arrow.
- **Text:** **ALL FOR $3?**
- **Why it works:** the scale contrast (thousands of cars vs one chip) creates curiosity, and it pays off the hook.

### D · "Taiwan's Grip" (pairs with title 5)
- **Visual:** A large hand-shaped shadow over a glowing Taiwan on a dark globe; India is lit in saffron below, with a chip icon rising from Gujarat.
- **Text:** **INDIA vs TAIWAN?**
- **Why it works:** a geopolitical rivalry frame. Use it carefully: the video's actual message is partnership (PSMC), so the question mark matters.

### Thumbnail checklist
- Face-free, but give it one strong focal object and keep the text away from the bottom-right (timestamp badge).
- Max 2 brand colours + white; saffron (#FF9933-ish) on near-black tests well against YouTube's white/dark UI.
- Make 1280×720 masters. Preview at 168×94 (mobile home feed) before you upload.
