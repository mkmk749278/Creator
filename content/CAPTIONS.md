# Captions and hashtags

Paste-ready copy for each rendered reel. Rules the copy follows:

* **The hook is the first line**, because Instagram truncates at roughly 125
  characters and everything after "… more" is read by almost nobody.
* **The risk line is included in full** on every post (see `COMPLIANCE.md`).
* **No performance claim, ever** — not in the caption either. The caption is the
  easiest place to break the rule and the only place nothing checks it.
* Hashtags: a small set, mixed reach. Thirty generic tags is 2019 advice and now
  reads as spam to both the ranker and the reader.

## Reply templates

Two questions will dominate the comments. Answer both the same way every time.

**"Does it guarantee profit?"**
> No, and anything that says it does is lying to you. Lumin gives you scored
> setups with a defined entry, stop and targets. Futures trading carries
> substantial risk of loss — paper mode exists so you can test it without money.

**"Do you take my funds / my keys?"**
> No. You connect Binance with a trade-only API key — withdrawal permission is
> never requested, and a key that has it is rejected. Funds stay in your own
> Binance account.

**Never answer a "what returns can I expect" question with a number.** Point at
paper mode instead.

60% of each rather than 28%. **The captions are written in the same voice** — a

is muted, which on Instagram is most of them.

and may never say *"it worked for us"*.
